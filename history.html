History of Irezumi

Irezumi is a traditional Japanese tattoo style that is often known for large, detailed designs that can cover major areas of the body. Although there are different names for Japanese tattooing, the word "irezumi" is commonly used and means "inserting ink." The different terms connected to Japanese tattooing can have slightly different meanings depending on the purpose of the tattoo and how it is viewed in society.

Japanese tattoos have had many meanings throughout history. In earlier periods, tattoos could be connected to spiritual beliefs, social identity, punishment, or marking criminals. Over time, tattooing developed into a more decorative and artistic form, especially through large designs that included powerful images and symbols.

Because of this history, tattoos in Japan have carried both positive and negative associations. They have sometimes been linked to criminality, but they are also recognized as an important form of art. Today, Irezumi is admired around the world for its bold designs, detailed artwork, and strong cultural symbolism. Many tattoo artists and tattoo styles continue to be influenced by traditional Japanese tattooing.
