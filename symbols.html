Irezumi Symbols and Meanings

Many Irezumi designs use images from nature, folklore, and traditional stories. The meaning of a tattoo can change depending on the full design, the direction of the subject, and the other symbols placed around it.

Symbol Common Meaning Design Notes
Dragon Wisdom, strength, protection, and power Often shown moving through clouds, wind, or water.
Koi Fish Courage, perseverance, and reaching goals Usually paired with waves or flowing water.
Tiger Strength, bravery, and protection from bad luck Often drawn with wind, bamboo, or rocky backgrounds.
Eagle Focus, freedom, watchfulness, and power Works well with clouds, wind, and open movement.
Flowers Beauty, change, season, and the passing of time Peonies, cherry blossoms, and chrysanthemums are common choices.

This table is a beginner guide. A complete Irezumi design should be researched carefully because traditional placement and symbol combinations can affect the final meaning.
