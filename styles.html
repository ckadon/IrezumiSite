Irezumi vs. Other Tattoo Styles

Irezumi is often recognized by large connected compositions, bold outlines, symbolic subjects, and flowing background elements. These features can make it feel different from smaller standalone tattoos or styles that focus mainly on realism, lettering, or minimal linework.

Other tattoo styles may emphasize a single image, a portrait-like effect, geometric patterning, or modern graphic simplicity. Irezumi usually depends on how the full design moves across the body and how each symbol works with the background.

Feature Irezumi Other Styles
Scale Often planned as a large connected composition. May be small, standalone, or arranged as separate pieces.
Imagery Uses traditional symbols, nature, folklore, and background flow. Can focus on portraits, lettering, abstraction, realism, or personal icons.
Composition Designed to move with the body and connect multiple elements. May prioritize one central image or a simpler isolated layout.
